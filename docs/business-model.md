# Business Model: Elementary Labour Site Scheduling Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9629`
- ISCO-08: `9629`
- Occupation: Elementary Workers Not Elsewhere Classified
- Social impact: worker-safety, site-safety, operational-continuity

## Classification note

ISCO-08 9629 is a residual "Not Elsewhere Classified" category covering
diverse manual/elementary labour work not captured by a more specific
ISCO 9xxx code (e.g. sweeping, messengers/porters, meter-reading/
collection, water/firewood collection each have their own dedicated
unit group; 9629 is everything else). This practice is deliberately
generic rather than tuned to one dominant hazard type.

## Customer

- facilities, event operators and organizations needing generic
  elementary-labour crews for varied manual/site tasks
- independent elementary-labour crews / labour cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- task/progress-record logging
- site-supplies procurement coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to site safety supervisors

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a labour-work-execution decision (e.g.
  finalizing a specific labour-work operation), ever
- no direct finalization of a site-safety-clearance decision (e.g.
  declaring a site safety cleared), ever
- no override of a site safety supervisor's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (manual-lifting/strain hazard, varied-site-
  condition hazard, outdoor/indoor exposure) always route to human
  sign-off, regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
